Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Zero Data Collection: Arc - Counter is a purely client-side front-end application. No metrics, personal details, or counter values are ever transmitted to an external server.

1. Local Storage Usage

We use standard HTML5 localStorage (`arc_counter_value`) to preserve your count value between sessions. This data resides exclusively on your local device.

2. Data Retention & Erasure

Your count data remains stored until you clear your web browser's cache or site data, or manually use the "Reset" action within the application.
